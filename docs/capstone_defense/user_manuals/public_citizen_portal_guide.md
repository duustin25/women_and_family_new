# 🌐 Public Citizen Portal & Services User Guide

> **Municipal & Barangay Women and Family Protection Information System (WFPIS)**  
> **Barangay 183, Villamor Airbase, Pasay City**

---

## 🏛️ 1. Resident Web Portal Overview

The WFPIS Public Portal provides residents of Barangay 183 with digital access to community programs, organization memberships, and 24/7 legal assistance without needing to line up at the barangay hall.

---

## 📝 2. Applying for Community Organization Membership

1. **Navigate to the Application Page:**
   - Visit the official portal and click **Join an Organization** (or navigate to `/membership/apply`).
2. **Select Target Organization:**
   - Choose from accredited associations:
     - *Solo Parents Association of Barangay 183* (under RA 11861)
     - *Persons with Disability (PWD) Federation* (under RA 7277)
     - *KALIPI Women's Empowerment Chapter*
     - *Senior Citizens Association* (under RA 9994)
     - *ERPAT Men's Support Group*
3. **Verify Your Email Address (OTP):**
   - Enter your personal email address and click **Send Verification Code**.
   - Open your email inbox, retrieve the 6-digit numeric token, and enter it into the portal within 10 minutes.
4. **Complete Profile & Upload Documents:**
   - Fill in full name, residential address/purok within Barangay 183, and contact number.
   - Attach clear photos or scans of supporting documents (e.g., Certificate of Solo Parenthood, Medical Certificate of Disability, or Proof of Barangay Residency).
5. **Receive Tracking Number:**
   - Upon submission, copy your unique reference code (e.g. `APP-2026-0412`) to monitor approval progress.

---

## ⚖️ 3. How to Appeal a Rejected Application

If your application was rejected by the organization president:
1. Open your rejection notification email.
2. Review the explanation provided in the `reject_reason` field.
3. Click the **File Appeal to Barangay Council** link within **15 calendar days**.
4. Attach an updated clear photo or explanation addressing the missing requirement.
5. The Barangay Council will review your appeal and issue an official decision.

---

## 🤖 4. Interacting with "The Sentinel" Virtual Assistant

- Click the purple floating chat icon at the bottom right of any public page.
- Ask questions regarding:
  - *"How do I file a Barangay Protection Order (BPO)?"*
  - *"What are the requirements for the Solo Parent ID?"*
  - *"When is the next community feeding or immunization drive?"*
  - *"Emergency police and rescue hotlines in Pasay City."*
- Click the speaker icon to listen to voice narration (Web Speech API).
